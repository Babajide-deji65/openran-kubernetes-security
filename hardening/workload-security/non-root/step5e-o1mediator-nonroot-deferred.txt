Date: Wed Sep  2 22:59:09 BST 2026

Decision: Non-root NOT applied
Reason 1: SSH host private keys at /etc/ssh/ssh_host_*_key are root:root 600
          These root-only keys present a compatibility concern for NETCONF-over-SSH
Reason 2: PID files are written under /run/ (root:root 755)
          netopeer2-server.pid and supervisord.pid
Reason 3: Supervisor logs are written to /var/log/supervisor/ (root:root 755)
          supervisord.conf configures logfile and pidfile in root-owned paths
Reason 4: supervisord runs as PID 1 and manages the Netopeer2 and O1Agent processes
          Moving the container to a non-root UID would require filesystem/runtime remediation
Note: TCP port 830 was assessed separately and was not itself a blocker because
      net.ipv4.ip_unprivileged_port_start=0 in the tested environment
